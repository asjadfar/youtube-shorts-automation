# Short — Flow Prompts (visual-only, portrait)

> One prompt per scene, in scene order. Every prompt starts with the style lock.
> Visual-only: no spoken words, no dialogue, no children speaking, no speech.
> The only text on screen is the single large numeral/letter for the scene being taught.
> Character descriptions are copied word for word from the Character lock — never change them.

## Style lock (start every prompt with this, word for word)

Soft 2D storybook cartoon, warm sunlight, painterly backgrounds, big expressive eyes, gentle soft colors, 2D cartoon style video, flat colorful cartoon characters with bold outlines, classic kids' cartoon look, 9:16 portrait vertical, 720x1280.

## Character descriptions (exact, used in every prompt)

- **Bunny:** a cute fluffy white bunny with long ears with pink insides, a small blue bow on one ear, big brown expressive eyes, a pink nose and rosy cheeks
- **Cow:** a black-and-white cow with a red collar and bell
- **Lion:** a friendly lion with a brown mane
- **Elephant:** a small grey elephant with pink ears
- **Dog:** a brown-and-white dog with a red collar
- **Duck:** a yellow duck
- **Pig:** a pink pig
- **All six friends:** a black-and-white cow with a red collar and bell, a friendly lion with a brown mane, a small grey elephant with pink ears, a brown-and-white dog with a red collar, a yellow duck, and a pink pig

## Frame check (before using any clip)

- Bunny on-model: white fluffy fur, long ears with pink insides, small blue bow on one ear, big brown eyes, pink nose, rosy cheeks.
- Friends on-model: cow black-and-white with red collar and bell, lion with brown mane, elephant grey with pink ears, dog brown-and-white with red collar, duck yellow, pig pink.
- No extra limbs, no burned-in text except the single numeral/letter for the scene, 9:16 portrait ratio (720x1280).
- The hook frame must show Bunny's face big and close — if Bunny is small or far away in the hook, regenerate it.
- Name errors plainly and regenerate the bad scene — do not ship it.
