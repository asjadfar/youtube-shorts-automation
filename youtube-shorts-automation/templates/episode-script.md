# Short Script — "Bunny Sounds" YouTube Short

> Fill in one copy of this template per Short. Keep every line brief and simple.
> Style lock for every visual prompt: soft 2D storybook cartoon, warm sunlight,
> painterly backgrounds, big expressive eyes, gentle soft colors,
> 2D cartoon style video, flat colorful cartoon characters with bold outlines,
> classic kids' cartoon look, 9:16 portrait vertical, 720x1280.
> Visual prompts are visual-only — no dialogue, no children speaking, no text on screen
> (except the single large numeral/letter for the scene being taught).
> Dialogue lives only in the voiceover column.
>
> LENGTH: every Short must be 60 seconds or less — 4-6 scenes of ~10 seconds each.
> Never deliver a 7-scene Short. Count the scenes before generating anything:
> hook (1) + learning beats (3-4) + finale (1) = 5-6 scenes = 50-60 seconds.
> A 4-scene Short (hook + 2 beats + finale) is the shortest allowed.

## Worked example — "Count 1 to 4 with Bunny" (6 scenes, 60 seconds)

- Scene 1 — Hook: Bunny in a sunny meadow, waving: "ہیلو! میں بنی ہوں! آؤ گنیں!" (Hello! Main Bunny hoon! Aao ginein!)
- Scene 2 — Number 1: One yellow duck in a sunny pond, big numeral "1": "کتنی بطخیں؟ ایک! ایک بطخ!" (Kitni batkhein? Ek! Ek batkh!)
- Scene 3 — Number 2: Two red apples on green grass, big numeral "2": "کتنے سیب؟ ایک، دو! دو سیب!" (Kitne saib? Ek, do! Do saib!)
- Scene 4 — Number 3: Three pink flowers in a sunny garden, big numeral "3": "کتنے پھول؟ ایک، دو، تین! تین پھول!" (Kitne phool? Ek, do, teen! Teen phool!)
- Scene 5 — Number 4: Four colorful balloons in a blue sky, big numeral "4": "کتنے غبارے؟ ایک، دو، تین، چار! چار غبارے!" (Kitne ghubaray? Ek, do, teen, chaar! Chaar ghubaray!)
- Scene 6 — Finale: Bunny and all six friends together: "بہت اچھا! سبسکرائب کریں!" (Bohat acha! Subscribe karein!)
